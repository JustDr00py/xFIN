## Full description (max 4000)

xFIN is a player for media you already have. Connect your own Jellyfin server and live TV playlists, and watch them on Android TV, phones and tablets in one app built around the TV remote.

xFIN does not include any channels, movies or shows. You need your own Jellyfin server, or a playlist from a provider you are licensed to use.

YOUR JELLYFIN LIBRARY
• Browse your movies and shows with artwork and details
• Continue watching and next up, synced with your Jellyfin server
• Plays the next episode automatically
• Choose between versions of a title when your server has more than one
• Search your whole library

LIVE TV
• Add M3U playlists with an XMLTV program guide
• A full program guide grid, plus a mini guide while you watch
• Catch-up on channels whose provider supports it
• Favorites, recent channels and channel groups you can show or hide
• Multiview: watch several channels at once

BUILT FOR THE BIG SCREEN
• Designed for the D-pad: every screen works with a TV remote
• Picture-in-picture on phones and tablets
• Subtitles, and a choice of how video fits the screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• No account with us. xFIN talks only to the servers you add
• Passwords are encrypted on your device
• Back up your sources and favorites to a password-protected file

Jellyfin is a trademark of its respective owner. xFIN is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
